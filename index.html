    1. Data Privacy & Collection:
    We deeply respect your privacy. Rehan Free Translator does not track, collect, or store any of your personal information, such as your name, email address, phone number, or geographical location. Your identity remains 100% anonymous.

    2. Local History Management:
    All your translation data and logs are saved strictly on your own local device storage. None of your inputs are uploaded to external databases or cloud servers. You retain absolute control over your logs and can clear your data permanently at any time via the 'Clear All' button in the History panel.

    3. Comprehensive Security:
    The application is designed focusing on client-side safety parameters. Since no remote data exploitation takes place, your converted sentences and texts stay fully confidential and completely isolated from third-party interventions.

    4. Policy Modifications:
    We may periodically update our Privacy Policy statement to maintain transparency. Feel free to review this section regularly for any minor optimizations.
