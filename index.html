Privacy Compliance Protocol

🛡️ 1. Zero Logs Commitment

Free Translator implements absolute cryptographic data segregation. Our network infrastructure, processing segments, and algorithmic clusters under no circumstances profile, parse, record, log, or maintain records of payloads, text input logs, dictionary search phrases, IP metrics, or cookie hashes onto centralized backend infrastructure databases.

⚖️ 2. GDPR & CCPA Compliance Matrix

System modules mirror constraints detailed within the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

  • Right of Erasure (GDPR Article 17): Translation and chat structures reside inside your local sandboxed execution stack. Tapping the clear functions updates state instances, rendering items permanently wiped.
  • Transient Caching Layers: String inputs remain stored in runtime volatile structures, which drop instantly the moment compute processes exit.

🎛️ 3. Client Storage & Cookie Limits

Our app only relies on local application keys (`localStorage`) to preserve your client configuration (such as dark mode preferences and recent dictionary states). These elements never transmit through server tunnels and can be purged from the browser configurations whenever needed. For escalation queries regarding user records, route administrative notifications directly to our intake inbox node: vahorarehan5510@gmail.com.
